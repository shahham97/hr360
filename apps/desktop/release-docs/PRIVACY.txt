HR360 PRIVACY POLICY SUMMARY

HR360 Systems ("HR360") is committed to protecting organizational data and personal privacy.

1. DATA COLLECTION
The HR360 application processes organizational employee records, attendance punches, and payroll information strictly on behalf of and according to instructions given by your subscribing employer ("Data Controller"). HR360 acts as a Data Processor.

2. LOCAL DATA STORAGE & ENCRYPTION AT REST
The desktop client stores local application preferences, encrypted authorization session tokens, and company workspace data within the secure user application data directory. All organizational records, employee information, attendance ledgers, and payroll calculations are cryptographically encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption anchored to your authenticated company license key. In the event of license expiration or device revocation, access to the encrypted data is locked until restored by an authorized administrator.

3. NETWORK TRANSMISSION
All communication between the HR360 desktop client and cloud services occurs over encrypted TLS 1.3 HTTPS connections. Direct communication with unencrypted third-party endpoints is strictly prohibited.

4. TELEMETRY & INTEGRITY
The application transmits essential device authorization signatures, client software versions, and security integrity status to ensure authorized tenant access and detect tampering.

For questions concerning your privacy or organizational data processing, contact privacy@hr360.com.
